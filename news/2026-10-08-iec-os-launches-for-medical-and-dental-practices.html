IEC OS launches for medical and dental practices

IEC has published the first two industry pages of IEC OS — one for medical practices, one for dental — covering MSO services, practice growth engines, and patient-access services now in pilot.

International Empire Consultants, Inc. has published the first two industry pages of IEC OS, its operating-system offering for healthcare organizations: one for medical practices and one for dental practices.

The medical page covers IEC's MSO services — staffing, revenue-cycle management and credentialing support — alongside patient-access services now in pilot, referral development, and marketing. The dental page focuses on practice growth: a new-patient engine, review engine, implant pipeline, recall and reactivation, and online booking.

Both pages are live on iecincglobal.com and linked in the site's main navigation. IEC OS for non-medical local business is next in the build sequence.
