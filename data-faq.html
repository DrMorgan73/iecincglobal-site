For owners
Your data, your terms

Owner questions.

Do I give up ownership of my data?

No. Licensing is never a sale: ownership stays with you at every step. A buyer receives defined rights for defined uses and a defined term — then returns or destroys the data.

What does the evaluation cost me?

Nothing. Completing the questionnaire and receiving buyer quotes costs nothing and commits you to nothing. You decide on real numbers, not promises.

What information do you need from me?

Counts only: volumes, date ranges, and the names of the systems you run. Never patient records, customer lists, or any personal information — none is needed, and none should ever be sent.

Will buyers know who I am?

No. The inventory is presented at network level — composition and counts, never an identified owner. Your participation is not disclosed to buyers.

Can I exclude a buyer?

Yes. You approve buyer categories in your grant, and you may exclude any buyer by name — no explanation needed. Exclusions are honored on every transaction.

What if I don't like the quote?

You decline it. A quote is an offer, not an obligation — terms are presented to you before anything is final, and nothing is licensed without your approval.

What is de-identification?

The process that removes every identifier from data before it can move — performed inside your own systems, under business associate agreements, and certified to the HIPAA Expert Determination standard.

Do I have to change my systems or software?

No. Member practices and companies keep their own systems. IEC MSO provides management services — the mandate covers data licensing, not tools. Nothing about your operation changes.

How does IEC get paid?

The economics are written into your exclusive grant and approved by you before anything is marketed. Buyers are quoted a single all-in license value, and your statements show exactly what was licensed, to whom, and what you were paid — with audit rights to verify it.

Can my data be licensed more than once?

Yes. Licensing is repeatable: the same data can be licensed to more than one buyer, so its value can be earned repeatedly instead of once.

For buyers
Principals, direct

Buyer questions.

Is IEC a broker?

No. IEC is the exclusive licensing counterparty: owners grant IEC the mandate, and IEC signs licenses in its own name. There is no intermediary between you and the counterparty — IEC is the counterparty.

Do you work through intermediaries?

No. IEC engages end buyers who sign, pay, and consume as principals. Brokers, marketplaces, and license-in / license-out intermediaries are not engaged — see the Principal Test.

Can I see the inventory?

Yes — under a mutual NDA. The written metadata inventory covers data classes, counts, languages, date ranges, and systems of origin, described at network level. Owners are never identified.

Can I get sample data?

Only after an NDA is executed, de-identification is complete, and the owner has approved the sample. That sequence protects your provenance as much as it protects the owner.

Is exclusivity available?

Buyer-side exclusivity can be structured — scoped to defined classes and time-boxed, and priced accordingly. The default license is non-exclusive.

What languages and specialties are covered?

The flagship is bilingual healthcare: clinical operations and documentation natively in English and Spanish, with English–Arabic depth, across multiple specialties staffed by certified physicians. Segment detail is in the inventory under NDA.
