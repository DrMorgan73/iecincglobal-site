Decision-ready coordination

Make the next decision easier to evaluate.

Complex opportunities lose momentum when responsibilities, facts and open questions are scattered. IEC organizes the business process so principals and advisers can focus on informed decisions.

  • Mandate, authority and stakeholder confirmation
  • Document and information-request organization
  • Commercial assumptions and issue tracking
  • Meeting preparation and decision summaries
  • Professional-adviser and counterparty coordination
