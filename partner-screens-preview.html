IEC PARTNER PORTAL

Work under IEC. Add your clients. Get paid on performance.

Partners work under IEC as IEC contractors. You add your clients to IEC's data-licensing program from your own portal, follow each client through the pipeline, and earn a referral fee that scales with the license — paid only when a license your client closes.

How it works

  1. 1Request access — your name, email and company. IEC records your request.
  2. 2Sign first — the IEC Contractor Referral Agreement and the NDA. Both signatures come before activation and before any client is added.
  3. 3IEC activates your portal — a one-time activation link by email. No shared logins, no passwords.
  4. 4Add your clients — profile + counts-only questionnaire. One partner per client: first to register wins.
  5. 5Track every client — questionnaire, assessment, NDA, registration.
  6. 6Get paid on performance — fee paid within 15 days after IEC is paid on the closed license.

Your fee is 5% of the owner's gross on a client's first closed license — the gross the owner receives — with no cap. At an owner gross of $10,000,000 or more, a 2% milestone bonus takes the total to 7%. Paid once per client, on their first closed license only (renewals earn no fee), within 15 days after IEC is paid. The fee is payable by IEC and is never deducted from any amount payable to the owner. Harbor Dental Group: owner gross $500,000 → fee $25,000 · paid.
