Healthcare growth & management

Build stronger healthcare organizations.

IEC supports medical and dental organizations with non-clinical growth strategy, MSO development, operational coordination, market expansion and healthcare concierge solutions.

Four disciplines

Healthcare-first, in practice.

IEC’s healthcare work concentrates in four disciplines — each one a substantive practice area, not a label.

Non-Clinical Growth

Growth strategy for medical and dental organizations that keeps clinical decision-making with licensed providers. Service-line planning, capacity analysis, referral-network development and market positioning — organized so operators can grow without compromising care.

MSO Planning

Design of management-service structures that separate business operations from clinical care. Organizational planning, administrative-service scope, staffing models and vendor coordination — developed alongside independent legal and compliance professionals.

Multi-Site Development

Repeatable non-clinical operating models for controlled expansion: location criteria, launch sequencing, staffing templates, referral playbooks and performance milestones that make each new site easier than the last.

Technology Commercialization

Practical paths for healthcare technology — from pilot design and workflow integration to institutional engagement and market development. Non-clinical business strategy for secure, human-reviewed platforms that fit real operations.

Non-clinical MSO development

Separate clinical care from business operations.

IEC helps design management-service structures that allow licensed providers to retain responsibility for patient care while a non-clinical organization supports business functions.

The MSO exists so clinicians can focus on care.
  • Organizational and ownership planning
  • Administrative-service scope
  • Staffing and vendor coordination
  • Technology and reporting workflows
  • Expansion and location planning
  • Coordination with independent legal and compliance professionals
Medical & dental scale-up

Support across the practice lifecycle.

Growth looks different for a new practice, an established group and a dental organization evaluating its next chapter. IEC adapts the engagement to the lifecycle stage — and coordinates the related real estate, staffing and technology decisions together, since they are rarely separable.

Healthcare concierge
