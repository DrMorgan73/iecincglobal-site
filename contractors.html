IEC Partner Portal

Private access for IEC partners.

This page is the entrance to IEC's private Partner Portal — the workspace for the partners who work under IEC as IEC contractors on the referral-fee program. The portal is not a public product: there are no usernames and no passwords, and entry is by personal activation link only, after the agreements are signed.

Partners — your entrance Request partner access

Door 1 — Partners: enter here

Use the activation link from your invitation — your portal opens there. The link is personal and one-time; it sets your access on your device for 30 days.

Lost your link, or it has expired? Write to info@iecincglobal.com and IEC will re-issue it.

If your link answers "signing required," your access is not open yet: activation begins only once IEC has recorded both of your signatures — the Contractor Referral Agreement and the NDA. IEC completes that step with you directly.

Door 2 — Request partner access

Applying to become an IEC partner? Send the request below. Sign-up is a request only — it creates no access. IEC reviews each request; the partner then signs the IEC Contractor Referral Agreement and the NDA (Combined Master), and IEC issues the personal activation link only once both signatures are recorded.

Your details go to IEC's office and are used only for the partner program. Nothing here asks for any client or patient information — never send individual records.

Next step — sign first. The Contractor Referral Agreement and the NDA (Combined Master) are already signed by IEC. Both of your signatures come before activation and before any detailed discussion.
