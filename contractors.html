IEC Partner Portal

Work under IEC. Add your clients. Get paid on performance.

Partners work under IEC as IEC contractors. You add your clients to IEC's data-licensing program from your own portal, follow each client through the pipeline, and earn a referral fee that scales with the license — paid only when a license your client closes. No license, no fee. You are an IEC contractor, not a producer, and not under any channel agreement.

How it works

  1. 1Request access below — your name, email and company. IEC records your request.
  2. 2Sign first — the IEC Contractor Referral Agreement and the NDA (Combined Master, already signed by IEC). Both signatures come before activation and before any client is added — no exceptions.
  3. 3IEC activates your portal — once both signatures are recorded, you receive a one-time activation link by email. No shared logins, no passwords.
  4. 4Add your clients — each client's business profile and counts-only questionnaire answers, in your portal. A client can be registered by one partner only: first to register wins.
  5. 5Track every client — questionnaire, assessment and valuation, NDA, registration — each step shows in your portal as it happens.
  6. 6Get paid on performance — when your client's first license closes, your fee is paid within 15 days after IEC is paid.
