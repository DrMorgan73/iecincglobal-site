Private access for IEC partners.

This page is the entrance to IEC's private Partner Portal — the workspace for the partners and contractors who work under IEC, across IEC's programs. The portal is not a public product: there are no usernames and no passwords, and entry is by personal activation link only, after the agreements are signed.

Common partner questions

How do I get into the portal?

Through your personal activation link — there are no usernames or passwords. IEC issues the link after both of your signatures are recorded, and it opens your portal on your device for 30 days. If it expires, IEC re-issues it.

What happens after I send the request form?

IEC reviews the request and contacts you about the agreements. You sign the IEC Contractor Referral Agreement and the NDA first; nothing is activated, and no client is added, before both signatures are on record.

What does a partner actually do?

You make introductions: you register the clients you know inside your portal, with each business's profile and counts-only questionnaire answers. IEC runs the assessment, the client's own agreements, and registration from there. You never handle client files, exports, records, or data.

Can I follow my clients' progress?

Yes. Every client's stage — questionnaire, assessment, agreements, registration — shows in your portal as IEC records it. You see your own clients only; no partner can see another partner's clients.

Can two partners register the same client?

No. A client can be registered by one partner only — the first registration holds, and a later one is declined without naming either side.

Where are the program's terms set out?

In the IEC Contractor Referral Agreement — the document you review and sign before activation. This page describes the process only; it publishes no terms, and nothing here is a promise of any outcome.
