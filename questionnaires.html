About data licensing

Licensing — and what your data is worth.

AI companies train their systems on real-world operational data — records, workflows, scheduling, transactions, billing patterns — and they pay to license it. A license is not a sale: you keep full ownership, the license covers defined uses for a defined time, and the same data can be licensed to more than one buyer.

Three things set the price: volume (how much data your operation produces), years of history (how deep your stored record runs), and standardization (the same workflows repeated across locations). Approved data partnerships in this market are published at $100K–$2M per partnership — larger, longer-running, multi-location operations price at the top of that range.

That is why every questionnaire asks for counts, date ranges, and system names only. Never send patient records, customer lists, or any personal or customer-identifying information — none is needed, and none should ever be sent. Anything that ever moves to a buyer is de-identified first, inside your own systems.
