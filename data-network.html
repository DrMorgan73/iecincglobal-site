Why IEC

A position no marketplace can rent.

AI buyers do not lack models — they lack rights-clean, de-identified real-world data with a provenance they never have to defend. The Network is built from the one seat that controls that data at the source: the management services organization inside the clinics.

Inside the clinics

IEC MSO runs the management layer of every member practice — billing, scheduling, systems — legally and daily, under business associate agreements. We do not rent access to clinics. We are already inside.

Exclusive mandate

Every member practice grants IEC MSO the exclusive right to license its data. One negotiation, one signature — buyers cannot go around the Network to the clinic.

One counterparty, many specialties

Assembling a multi-specialty dataset the ordinary way means dozens of providers and dozens of agreements. The Network answers with a single agreement covering the whole pool.

Bilingual by birth

The founding patient community is majority Latino, so Spanish-language clinical documentation is native to this data — documented in Spanish, not translated into it. Multilingual corpora of this kind barely exist in the U.S. market at any price.

Clean chain of rights

Practice grant → MSO mandate → HIPAA de-identification → use-limited buyer license. Every step documented, every license traceable to a written grant.

For practices
Your data is an asset

Your records already hold value. The Network puts it to work — on your terms.

Your practice generates clinical and operational data every day. Licensed properly — de-identified, rights-clean, under strict buyer covenants — that data earns. The Network handles the entire process; you keep ownership and control throughout.

01

Sign the schedule

Your MSO services agreement gains a Data Licensing Schedule: an exclusive mandate to IEC MSO, with your approval rights over buyer categories written in.

02

De-identified inside your systems

Data is de-identified to the HIPAA standard before anything moves. Zero protected health information ever leaves your practice — de-identified data only, ever.

03

IEC licenses to vetted buyers

IEC MSO licenses your de-identified data directly to vetted AI buyers under defined-use licenses with strict no-re-identification covenants.

04

Statements & shared revenue

You receive regular statements showing exactly what was licensed, to whom, and for what — and your practice shares in the licensing revenue.

  • Ownership never transfers. Your data remains yours; buyers receive defined rights for defined uses for a defined term — a license, never a sale.
  • You approve the buyers. Every practice approves buyer categories, and any practice may exclude a named buyer.
  • Full transparency. Audit rights on every statement; you always know what was licensed, to whom, and for what purpose.

Covenants
