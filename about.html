Relationships, strategy and disciplined execution.

IEC is a Boston-based business-development and strategic consulting company working across healthcare, technology, real estate and international markets.

Our company

A connective platform for growth.

IEC helps clients move from an ambitious idea to an organized, credible path forward. We define the opportunity, align stakeholders, develop relationships and coordinate execution.

Our cross-sector model is intentional. Many significant opportunities sit between industries — healthcare and real estate, innovation and institutions, international markets and local execution. IEC works at those intersections.

How IEC creates value

More than an introduction.

IEC combines relationship development with the preparation and follow-through required to turn a promising conversation into an actionable opportunity.

  • Clarify the mandate, audience and desired outcome
  • Shape a credible business narrative and engagement strategy
  • Identify aligned decision-makers, partners and advisers
  • Coordinate meetings, information requests and next steps
  • Maintain discretion across sensitive multi-party discussions
