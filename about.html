Relationships, strategy and disciplined execution.

IEC is a Boston-based business-development and strategic consulting company working across healthcare, technology, real estate and international markets.

Our company

A connective platform for growth.

IEC helps clients move from an ambitious idea to an organized, credible path forward. We define the opportunity, align stakeholders, develop relationships and coordinate execution.

Our cross-sector model is intentional. Many significant opportunities sit between industries — healthcare and real estate, innovation and institutions, international markets and local execution. IEC works at those intersections.

IEC works at the intersection of industries, institutions and markets.
Our story

Founded in Boston in 2016.

International Empire Consultants, Inc. was founded by Dr. Mohammed M. Morgan to bring a relationship-driven, cross-sector approach to business development — one shaped by more than two decades across healthcare and international business.

The model is deliberately connective. Significant opportunities rarely sit inside a single industry: a medical group needs real estate and technology; an international investor needs local healthcare expertise; a technology company needs institutional relationships. IEC was built to work at those intersections, coordinating strategy, people and execution until the opportunity becomes real.

Today the firm leads with healthcare — growth, partnerships and non-clinical management support for medical and dental organizations — while continuing to serve clients across technology, real estate and international commerce.

How IEC creates value

More than an introduction.

IEC combines relationship development with the preparation and follow-through required to turn a promising conversation into an actionable opportunity.

  • Identify aligned decision-makers, partners and advisers
  • Coordinate meetings, information requests and next steps
  • Maintain discretion across sensitive multi-party discussions

Founder & CEO

Dr. Mohammed M. Morgan, M.D.

International business development and strategic partnerships

Dr. Morgan leads IEC’s healthcare growth, international business development and strategic partnership initiatives. His work combines clinical understanding, commercial strategy and a relationship-driven international network.

He earned his M.B.Ch.B. at the University of Alexandria, completed a cardiology post-doctoral fellowship at Massachusetts General Hospital / Harvard Medical School, and served as an endocrine research fellow at Massachusetts General Hospital. His operating career spans more than 20 years across healthcare delivery, medical business development and international ventures. He works in English and Arabic.

From Boston, he works with healthcare leaders, innovators, operators, investors and international partners to help promising opportunities reach the right audience and move toward execution.
