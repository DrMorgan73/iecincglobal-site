Partners — Partner Portal Admin

Partners work under IEC as IEC contractors on the referral-fee program. They are not producers and not under any channel: their pay is a performance-based referral fee, only when a license a client they added closes. Partners never hold a Desk login — they enter through a one-time activation link, and activation opens only once both signatures are recorded: the Contractor Referral Agreement and the NDA. A partner sees only his own clients. Client pipeline steps are recorded here, one at a time, from the real events — with the evidence noted.
