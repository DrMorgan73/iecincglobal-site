The demo blueprint

Every future demo follows the same model as MEDEXEC and InSnap: a provisioning store as the single source of truth, a one-time activation link (the signer sets their own password — passwords are never emailed), the invited / active / paused / revoked status ladder, and a login notification to DrMorgan on every activation and sign-in. When a new asset's demo is built, it plugs into this portal with one registry entry — no new control panel.
