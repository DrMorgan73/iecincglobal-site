MOTION ARENA · PING PONG

First to 11, win by 2. On the Samsung TV: open the Internet browser, come to this page, and pick TV Host — the remote's arrow keys and Enter play the whole game. For phone controllers, open the controller page on your phone and type the room code the TV shows.

📺 TV Host
Open this on the TV or any big screen. Shows the table, the score and the room code. Playable with remote / keyboard / mouse alone.
📱 Phone Controller
Open this on your phone, enter the room code from the TV, and play with touch-drag or tilt. Needs internet so the phone can reach the TV.

No internet? The game still plays on one screen — 1 Player vs Computer or 2 Players sharing it. See README.txt in the download for the USB-stick and Android app routes.
