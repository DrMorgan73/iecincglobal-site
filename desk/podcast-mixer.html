Level guide

Aim for −12 dB. Normal speech should peak in the yellow, around −12 to −6 dB. Occasional red peaks are fine; a lit CLIP light means turn that channel down.

Gate cuts the mic between sentences so room noise and the other speaker's bleed stay out. Raise the threshold until quiet moments go silent, then back off slightly.

No mic handy? Each channel has a Test Tone source so you can learn the board — faders, EQ, pan, and meters all respond.
