💾 Take it with you

Download the game for a USB stick or a Samsung Galaxy phone/tablet. A Samsung TV cannot install from USB — on the TV, always use the web address above.

Download USB package (.zip) Download Android app (.apk)

ZIP: unzip anywhere, open index.html in a browser — 1-player and same-screen 2-player work with no internet at all. APK: copy to a Galaxy device, open it in Files and Install (allow installs from that source when Android asks).

📺 On the Samsung TV

  1. Open the TV's Internet browser and come to this Desk page (sign in if asked), then choose Open TV Host.
  2. 1 Player vs Computer needs nothing else: the remote's ↑ / ↓ arrows move your paddle and Enter starts, serves and restarts.
  3. For phone play, open Phone Controller on the phone (same Desk sign-in), type the room code on the TV, and tap Join.
