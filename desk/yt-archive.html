How YT Archive works

This page is the queue and the board — nothing is downloaded in the cloud. The runner on DrMorgan's Mac polls for queued jobs with its sync secret, claims one, and downloads it with yt-dlp into ~/Downloads/youtube-archive — best quality merged to MP4, files named by date and title, resumable if it ever stops. While it works it reports how many videos are done, how many bytes are down, and the title it is on; when it finishes, the full catalog (date, title, duration) lands on the job above. The sync secret lives only in this project's settings and in the runner's local config — never in this page. Public videos need no account; unlisted videos work from their direct links; private videos cannot be fetched without account access.
