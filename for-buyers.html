What IEC licenses

Data classes, at network level.

Composition and counts are shared as a written metadata inventory under NDA — never on a public page. At network level, the classes are:

Bilingual clinical documentation

Clinical documentation produced natively in Spanish by the clinicians delivering the care — documented in Spanish, never translated into it — alongside English documentation and an English–Arabic channel. Authentic bilingual corpora of this kind barely exist on the open market.

Multi-specialty clinical data

Encounter and clinical documentation, procedure records, and longitudinal follow-up across multiple specialties, produced by certified physicians — de-identified to the HIPAA Expert Determination standard.

Imaging & longitudinal records

Imaging volumes where applicable, referral and scheduling patterns, and longitudinal patient-journey records that follow care over years, not episodes.

Operational data, beyond healthcare

The same mandate covers operational data in the Network's other segments — pharmacy, home health, therapy, dental, retail fuel operations, real estate, staffing, and professional services.

Who IEC licenses to

The Principal Test.

IEC licenses to principals — end buyers who consume data in their own products and models. Three questions decide every engagement:

01

Do you sign?

You sign the license in your own name, as the buyer of record — not on behalf of an unnamed client, fund, or marketplace.

02

Do you pay?

You pay the license value as principal, on the license's terms — not a share of whatever you can resell downstream.

03

Do you consume?

The data trains, validates, or improves your own products and models. If it is destined for onward licensing, this is not your door.

Brokers, marketplaces, facilitators, and license-in / license-out intermediaries are not engaged — at any tier, on any terms. If the answer to all three questions is yes, the Network is built for you: one mandate, one counterparty, and provenance you never have to defend.

Provenance

A clean chain of rights.

  • Owner grant. Every member of the Network has signed an exclusive data licensing grant to IEC — consent and rights are documented at the source, before any buyer conversation.
  • IEC mandate. IEC licenses in its own name as exclusive counterparty. Your license traces to the owners' grants without your ever needing to assemble dozens of provider agreements.
  • De-identification. Data is de-identified inside the owner's own systems, under business associate agreements, before any delivery — Expert Determination under HIPAA.
  • Flow-down covenants. Every license carries the same terms: defined use only, no re-identification, no linkage attacks, no onward transfer, security obligations, and return or certified destruction at term end.

How engagement works.
