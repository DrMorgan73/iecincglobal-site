Healthcare-first

Four disciplines for stronger healthcare organizations.

IEC leads with healthcare. Our work with medical and dental organizations concentrates in four areas:

  • Non-clinical growth — service-line planning, referral development and market positioning that respect clinical independence.
  • MSO planning — management-service structures that separate business operations from clinical decision-making.
  • Multi-site development — repeatable operating models for controlled regional expansion.
  • Technology commercialization — practical paths that move healthcare technology from pilot to adoption.
