About data licensing

Licensing — and what your data is worth.

AI companies train their systems on real-world operational data — records, workflows, scheduling, transactions, billing patterns — and they pay to license it. A license is not a sale: you keep full ownership, the license covers defined uses for a defined time, and the same data can be licensed to more than one buyer, so value can be earned repeatedly. Three things set the price: volume, years of history, and standardization — the same workflows repeated across locations.

Approved licensing programs in this market range from $100,000 to $5,000,000 per term. Your share of any license IEC closes on your data is 80% of the license value, paid when the buyer pays.

How it works — five steps

  1. Register your company below and complete the short questionnaire — counts only.
  2. IEC reviews your counts and values the opportunity.
  3. You review and sign IEC's data licensing agreements — nothing is licensed without your signature.
  4. IEC registers and presents your company to buyers; any data that ever moves is de-identified first, inside your own systems.
  5. You are paid your 80% when a license closes and the buyer pays.

Counts only, always. The questionnaire asks for totals, years of history and system names — never individual records, never customer or patient information.
