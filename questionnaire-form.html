About data licensing

Licensing — and what your data is worth.

AI companies train their systems on real-world operational data — records, workflows, scheduling, transactions, billing patterns — and they pay to license it. A license is not a sale: you keep full ownership, the license covers defined uses for a defined time, and the same data can be licensed to more than one buyer, so value can be earned repeatedly. Three things set the price: volume, years of history, and standardization — the same workflows repeated across locations. Approved data partnerships in this market are published at $100K–$2M per partnership.

The process: (1) you complete this questionnaire — counts only; (2) you sign IEC's exclusive data licensing grant, which makes IEC your sole counterparty to buyers (no brokers, no marketplaces); (3) buyers evaluate the counts and quote; (4) any data that ever moves is de-identified first, inside your own systems; (5) you see the terms before anything is final, and nothing is licensed without the agreement you signed. Your share of any license IEC closes on your data is 75% of the license value, paid when the buyer pays.
